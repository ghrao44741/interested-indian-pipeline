checkpoint1_v1_provisional — archived 2026-08-01

These are the v1 canonical alternate views and expressions generated during
Character Checkpoint 1. They are retained for reference and comparison.

NOT APPROVED. Do NOT use any file in this directory as an anchor for the pose
library or for future generation. They were produced before the face master
existed, and their framing was wrong: the "expressions" came back as full-body
figures rather than head-and-shoulders portraits, so they cannot serve as an
expression reference.

The approved anchors are recorded in character/character_spec.json under
"masters". At time of archiving that is body_master only; face_master is pending.
