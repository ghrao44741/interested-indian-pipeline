HUMOR_SCORE: 4/10

RHYTHM_SCORE: 7/10

AUDIENCE_SCORE: 6/10

But earlier sections miss obvious viewer questions: "Wait, why don't they just run the exam twice a year?" or "Is this just an India problem?" The script never names those doubts.

WITTINESS_SCORE: 6/10
When it lands, it lands hard (the cockroach-as-brand section is genuinely funny). But too much of this reads like a well-written Scroll.in longread. I'd watch at 1x for the first four minutes, then start drifting.

EVENHANDEDNESS_SCORE: 7/10

But the phrase "protesters said they were standing with placards and got charged anyway" slightly tips the framing by putting the gentler claim second (recency bias). The "anti-national" line could also use a beat of "some protesters did block roads, which is technically illegal" before dismissing the government's optics failure. Not unfair, but not perfectly calibrated either.

OVERALL_SCORE: 6.5/10
This is a B+ investigative feature that forgot it's supposed to be entertainment. The research is solid, the story is there, but it's too sober for too long. Needs 5-6 more humor beats and tighter rhythm in the middle third.
