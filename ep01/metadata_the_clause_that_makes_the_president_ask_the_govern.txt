The Bizarre Way India's Prime Minister Fires State Governments

# VIDEO DESCRIPTION:
If the PM wants to dismiss a state government, the President has to ask the Governor to suggest the Chief Minister maybe resign. This is real. This has happened 91 times. And the constitutional loophole behind it is somehow even weirder than it sounds.

I spent a week reading about Article 356 — India's "President's Rule" provision — and how it's been used to dismiss elected state governments for reasons ranging from actual emergencies to "the national mood changed." The mechanics are absurd. The history is messier. And the whole thing hinges on one constitutional phrase that nobody bothered to define.
