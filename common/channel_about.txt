India runs on rules nobody explained to you, run by institutions nobody named for you, doing things nobody quite understands — and I got tired of that.

The Interested Indian breaks down how the country actually works: the exam agency that's supposed to stop cheating, the emergency law that lets the President fire a state government, the film industry politics playing out behind the scenes, the temple trust managing more money than most companies. No lecture voice, no jargon left untranslated — just the weird, specific, occasionally absurd mechanics of a billion-person country, explained like I'm telling a friend over chai.

New episodes when they're ready, not on a content-calendar schedule — I'd rather ship something good than ship something on time.
